My Journey from Beginner to Developer

Hey, my name is Zach, and the start of my programming journey is probably similar to a lot of people before me. I originally wanted to learn a programming language because I wanted to develop my own game. I started with C#, and to be fair, I actually made it a little ways in, but things got complicated fast and I ended up quitting. I repeated that cycle several times with different languages before finally landing where I am today.

My biggest problem was that I kept trying to skip ahead. I wanted to dive straight into the hard stuff without truly learning the fundamentals I needed first. So here I am now, building this blog from the ground up because I decided to start fresh with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript alongside Python. Starting from the beginning instead of jumping ahead has helped me more than I can explain.

But if I’m being honest, the real reason I’m in a better place than before is because of my wife. Her name is Miracle, and she decided to learn programming too and it’s amazing how well she’s been doing, even if she doesn’t believe it herself. She’s been keeping me motivated, and not just because I’m extremely competitive haha.

I’ll be sharing much more about my journey here on this blog, but I wanted to put this intro up to say one thing: if you truly want to learn to program but you keep quitting, remember that the only thing standing in your way is you. Keep pushing forward no matter what, and one day you’ll be exactly where you want to be.
